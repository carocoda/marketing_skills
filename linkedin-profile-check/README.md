# LinkedIn Profile Check

A skill for ChatGPT and Claude that checks your LinkedIn profile the way a buyer sees it, and helps you fix it. Your profile is your sales page, and three places do most of the selling: your headline, your banner and your Featured section.

**What it does**

- Asks you a few questions first: what you sell, the one thing you want visitors to do, and how far you want to go
- Looks at your profile, through your link or two screenshots
- Gives you clear feedback on your headline, banner and Featured section, with one fix for each
- Writes three new headline options and tells you which one it would pick
- Asks about your colours, fonts and look, and which photos and logo you have
- Writes ready-to-use image prompts for a new banner (two directions) and your Featured images, based on your own photos and logo
- Plans your first week if you want: three content pillars, one conversion pillar, and a Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday post idea

It never touches your LinkedIn account. You make every change yourself.

## Use it

**ChatGPT, any plan (Free included)**
1. Download `linkedin-profile-check.md`.
2. Open a new chat, drag the file in and type: "Use this skill to check my LinkedIn profile."
3. Answer its questions. Have your profile link or two screenshots ready (the top of your profile and your Featured section), plus your headshot and logo.

On a Business, Enterprise or Edu plan you can also install it: Plugins > Skills > Create > Upload, and upload the ZIP.

**Claude** (web, desktop or mobile; every plan, Free included)
1. Download `linkedin-profile-check.zip` and keep it zipped.
2. Go to Settings > Capabilities and switch on **Code execution and file creation**.
3. Go to Customize > Skills > **+** > Create skill > **Upload a skill**, and choose the ZIP.
4. Open a new chat and say: "Check my LinkedIn profile."

Claude doesn't make images, so it gives you the banner and Featured prompts to paste into ChatGPT.

**Claude Code:** copy the `linkedin-profile-check` folder into `.claude/skills/` (or `~/.claude/skills/`) and restart. **Codex:** put the folder in `~/.codex/skills/`.

## Good to know

- Screenshots always work. A profile link only works if LinkedIn lets the AI open the page, and it often asks for a login.
- Image tools sometimes misspell words or change how a face looks. Check every word, and if the photo doesn't look like you, add your real photo afterwards in a design tool like Canva.
- Nothing gets made up: results, clients and logos only come from you.
