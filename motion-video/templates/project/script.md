# Script: the template video (an example of what the gate sends)

16:9, 32.0 s, 120 BPM, 16 bars (64 beats), loops. Voice: Gemini TTS, voice Aoede (an example). Music: the
placeholder loop until a real track is picked.

## The story

A tile is clicked and the promise slams in. One shape then shows what the product does, each step operated by a
cursor: a prompt is typed and rendered, a toggle puts a slide in the brand style, a chart adds up, a workflow ticks
off the week. The button asks the question, turns into the call to action, and the last click goes back to the tile.

## Cost estimate (what the gate message says)

Voice: 6 lines × 2 takes, plus 4 extra takes for hard words, about $0.05. Music: one Lyria song, $0.08, maybe a
second try. About $0.15 to $0.25 in total. Or $0 with the scratch voice and your own track.
